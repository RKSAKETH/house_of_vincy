      Privacy Policy

      House of Vincy (“House of Vincy,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our customers and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, make purchases, or interact with our services.

      1. Information We Collect

      We may collect the following types of information:

      • Personal Information – Name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing addresses, and other details necessary for order processing.
      • Payment Information – Payment details are collected when you make a purchase, but we do not store your credit card or payment information. Payments are securely processed through third-party payment providers.
      • User Account Information – Login credentials, order history, and preferences if you create an account with us.
      • Communication Information – Data related to your interactions with us, including emails, feedback, or product reviews.
      • Website Usage Information – IP address, browser details, and browsing behavior on our website.

      2. How We Use Your Information

      We use your information for the following purposes:

      • To Process Orders – Using personal and payment details to process and fulfill your purchases.
      • Account Management – Managing user accounts, tracking order history, and providing a personalized shopping experience.
      • Communication – Sending order updates, customer support responses, and promotional offers. You can opt out of marketing emails at any time.
      • Website Improvements – Analyzing website usage data to improve performance, content, and user experience.

      3. Information Sharing

      We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. However, we may share your data with:

      • Service Providers – Trusted third-party partners for payment processing, order fulfillment, and website maintenance.
      • Legal Compliance – Government authorities or legal processes when required by law.

      4. Your Choices

      You have the right to:

      • Review and Update Your Information – Access and update personal details by logging into your account.
      • Opt Out of Marketing Communications – Unsubscribe from promotional emails by following the instructions in the email.

      5. Security

      We implement security measures to protect your personal information. However, no online data transmission or storage method is completely secure. While we take precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

      6. Changes to the Privacy Policy
